# MAYHEM Information Advantage Audit

## Purpose
The NRL MAYHEM is also a controlled information-asymmetry laboratory. It tests when ordinary public information becomes decision-useful because an actor can continuously collect, connect, timestamp and remember it.

## Experimental chain
Public fragments → aggregation → corroboration → inference → information advantage → decision → measurable outcome.

## Required separation
Source facts, MAYHEM inference, market response, decision effect and post-match interpretation must remain separately identifiable. A later outcome must never be used to rewrite the pre-match state.

## Per-signal record
Record: source; source class; publication/observation timestamp; capture timestamp; public accessibility; evidence classification; information fragment; corroborating sources; contradictions; inferred meaning; confidence effect; observed market state; decision effect; checkpoint; frozen state.

## Information-advantage classification
- NONE — no evidence the information improved the decision state.
- POSSIBLE — plausible advantage but insufficient independent evidence.
- CORROBORATED — multiple independent observations support a decision-relevant advantage.
- DEMONSTRATED — frozen pre-match evidence plus subsequent validation demonstrates measurable decision value.

A correct match result alone is insufficient to classify an advantage as demonstrated.

## Market absorption test
For each material signal compare its timestamp against observable head-to-head, line and relevant player-market movement. Record whether the market appears to have incorporated the information before MAYHEM, after MAYHEM, contemporaneously, or not detectably.

## Fragmentation test
Record whether a signal was directly useful or became useful only after joining multiple otherwise innocuous public observations. Preserve the fragments so the aggregation effect can be audited.

## Decision-effect classes
- NO EFFECT
- CONFIDENCE CHANGE
- RANKING CHANGE
- TIP CHANGE
- ABSTAIN / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

## Post-match validation
After full time, grade the locked tip separately from the intelligence hypothesis. Review which signals were predictive, misleading, redundant or already market-absorbed. Do not alter frozen pre-match evidence.

## Longitudinal source performance
Measure each source class by availability, corroboration rate, contradiction rate, decision influence, predictive contribution and redundancy with the market. Source weights may change only from accumulated evidence.

## Integrity rule
No retroactive tipping. No hindsight reconstruction presented as pre-match knowledge. Public availability does not itself establish reliability, independence or decision value.
