# MAYHEM NRL Source Priority and Performance

Canonical governance snapshot: GOVERNANCE-2026-09-06-TI-001

The labels below are **research-priority priors**, not empirical reliability weights. Empirical source performance is maintained separately in `data/source-performance-ledger-2026.json` and may change only from accumulated audited evidence.

1. Primary official — highest research priority for team lists, official late mail, judiciary, club availability updates, official withdrawals, fixtures, results and published statistics.
2. Specialist reporting — high discovery priority for training observations, local reporting and role context; material claims should return to primary evidence where possible.
3. Market observations — high temporal priority for prices and movement; market state is not independent football evidence and cannot establish causation for a move.
4. Club/community intelligence — medium discovery priority; corroboration required for material factual treatment.
5. Fantasy/SuperCoach communities and derived datasets — medium hypothesis-generation priority; preserve methodology and never equate fantasy scoring with direct football performance.
6. Weak social signals — low; anonymous or indirect social claims cannot independently determine a tip.
7. Environmental intelligence — context dependent; official weather is preferred where available and directional football effects require a mechanism.
8. Historical officiating data — low-to-medium and only directional when a sufficiently credible repeated effect exists.

## Canonical source classes

Persistent evidence should use the canonical classes in `data/source-class-registry.json` while preserving the original encountered class for historical migration. Aliases are mapped, not silently rewritten.

## Performance rule

Source performance is assessed longitudinally by capture availability, corroboration rate, contradiction rate, decision influence, predictive contribution, redundancy with market information and temporal usefulness. Small samples remain explicitly small-sample states. A source cannot be upgraded or downgraded merely because one tip won or lost.
