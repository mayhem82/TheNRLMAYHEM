# MAYHEM NRL Source Weighting

1. Primary official — very high: NRL team lists, official late mail, judiciary, club injury updates and official withdrawals.
2. Specialist reporting — high: established club reporters, Fantasy/SuperCoach late mail, training observers and local sports journalists.
3. Market intelligence — medium-high: head-to-head movement, line movement, player-prop suspension and cross-bookmaker divergence.
4. Club/community intelligence — medium: reserve-grade watchers, club-specific forums and supporter groups.
5. Fantasy/SuperCoach communities — medium: expected minutes, role changes, bench rotations and reserve-list interpretation.
6. Weak social signals — low: player/family posts, travel clues, deleted posts and anonymous claims. These cannot independently determine a tip.
7. Environmental intelligence — context dependent: weather, surface, wind, humidity, travel, turnaround and representative-football backup.
8. Historical officiating data — low-medium: referee tendencies only when a statistically credible effect is demonstrated.

Post-match calibration may alter future practical weighting, but never rewrites the historical pre-match evidence record.
