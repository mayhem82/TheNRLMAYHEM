Road to the Premiership — Every Team

Every club remains visible from Round 1 until its premiership road closes. The intelligence layer explains what must happen next, what other results matter, whether a loss can be survived, and how many finals wins remain between the club and the NRL Premiership.

How the finals road works

1st–4th: Qualifying Final. Win and the club skips Week 2, then plays a Preliminary Final. Lose in Week 1 and the club remains alive through a Semi-final.

5th–8th: Elimination Final. A Week 1 loss ends the season. The winner must then survive a Semi-final, Preliminary Final and Grand Final.

Minor Premiership: finishing first after the regular season. It is separate from winning the NRL Premiership. A club can miss the Minor Premiership and still become NRL Premier.
