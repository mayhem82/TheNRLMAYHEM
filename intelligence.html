What MAYHEM records
VERIFIEDUNVERIFIEDCONTRADICTEDSIGNAL

Every observation keeps its source, publication time, capture time, evidence state and match context. Later information adds a new state; it does not erase the earlier one.

Change Detection

MAYHEM compares successive public-information states and elevates the change itself as intelligence.

ADDED
New team-list item, injury update, market state, specialist report or other observation appears.
REMOVED
An item previously present disappears from the later public state.
CHANGED
A material field changes: selection, injury status, role, price, venue, kickoff or other decision-relevant fact.
Conflict Register

Contradictions remain visible until resolved. MAYHEM does not silently choose whichever version fits the current tip.

Information Advantage

PUBLIC FRAGMENTS → AGGREGATION → INFERENCE → ADVANTAGE → DECISION → OUTCOME

Correct tipping is not itself evidence of information advantage. The audit tests whether fragmented public information, assembled before the market or ordinary observer had fully absorbed it, materially changed the decision state.
