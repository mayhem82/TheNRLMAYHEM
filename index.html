Tipping Intelligence Layer
1 · Primary official evidence — VERY HIGH

NRL team lists, official late mail, judiciary, club injury updates, withdrawals and final teams.

2 · Statistical evidence — HIGH

Season/recent form, attack/defence, opponent context, venue, rest and availability.

3 · Specialist reporting — HIGH

Club reporters, credible local journalists, training observers and specialist late-mail reporters.

4 · Market intelligence — MEDIUM–HIGH

Opening/current H2H and line, movement magnitude/velocity, cross-bookmaker agreement and timing against news.

5 · Club/community intelligence — MEDIUM

Reserve-grade watchers, club communities and supporter networks; corroboration required.

6 · Fantasy/SuperCoach — MEDIUM

Expected minutes, roles, bench rotation, injury-return workload and reserve-list interpretation.

7 · Environmental — CONTEXT DEPENDENT

Weather, wind, humidity, surface, travel, turnaround and representative-football backup.

8 · Weak social signals — LOW

Player/family posts, travel clues, deleted posts and anonymous claims remain UNVERIFIED without corroboration.

9 · Officiating — LOW–MEDIUM

Only statistically established referee effects; unsupported folklore rejected.

Evidence classification
VERIFIED

UNVERIFIED

Potentially relevant claim without sufficient support.

CONTRADICTED

Conflicts with stronger evidence or subsequent confirmation.

SIGNAL

Indicator worth tracking without asserting its underlying cause.
