Information Advantage Audit

PUBLIC FRAGMENTS → AGGREGATION → INFERENCE → ADVANTAGE → DECISION → OUTCOME

This layer measures whether combining ordinary public information creates a measurable decision advantage beyond any individual source.

1 · Information availability
Record what was publicly knowable, its publication timestamp, source class, accessibility and whether it was broadly visible or fragmented across sources.
2 · Fragmentation
Record whether the signal was useful alone or only became meaningful after combination with other observations. Preserve the independent fragments.
3 · Aggregation and inference
Record the inference MAYHEM produced from combined evidence, supporting fragments, contradictions and confidence change. Inference remains distinguishable from source fact.
4 · Market absorption
Compare inferred-signal timing with observable market movement.
5 · Information advantage
Classify apparent advantage as NONE, POSSIBLE, CORROBORATED or DEMONSTRATED. A correct result alone does not establish advantage.
6 · Decision effect
Record whether intelligence changed selected team, confidence, ranking or no decision.
7 · Outcome validation
Test the frozen pre-match inference against the result without rewriting the frozen record.
8 · Source performance
Measure source classes longitudinally and reweight only from accumulated evidence.
