Method boundary

Implemented signals use only fixture identity and verified results from earlier rounds. Same-round results are not allowed to affect another fixture in that round. Historical market, lineup, travel, weather and other candidates remain pending until genuine pre-match observations can be sourced without leakage.

Any signal promoted beyond retrospective association must later be frozen prospectively and evaluated without changing its rule.
